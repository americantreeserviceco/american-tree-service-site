About Us

At American Tree Service, we are dedicated to providing top-notch tree care services to homeowners and businesses across the Colorado region. Our team of experienced arborists and tree service professionals are passionate about what they do and are committed to maintaining the health and beauty of your landscape.

Our Mission

Our mission is to offer reliable, efficient, and environmentally responsible tree care solutions. We believe that a well-maintained tree can enhance the value of your property and improve the overall aesthetic of your surroundings. With our expertise and commitment, we aim to exceed your expectations and deliver the best possible service.

Our Services

Tree Trimming and Pruning

We help keep your trees healthy and well-shaped by removing dead, diseased, or crossing branches.

Tree Removal

Our professional team safely removes trees that are hazardous, overcrowded, or no longer desired.

Stump Removal

We provide efficient stump grinding and removal to ensure your property looks neat and tidy.

Tree Planting

We offer expert advice on tree selection and planting to help you add new trees to your landscape.

Emergency Tree Services

In case of storm damage or other emergencies, we respond quickly to restore your trees to good health.
